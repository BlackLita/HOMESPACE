# Home on a Promise

> A first-person sci-fi exploration and survival game about maintaining a damaged spaceship and finding your way back home.

## About

**Home on a Promise** is a single-player first-person game where the player controls the last surviving crew member of a damaged spacecraft.

Your mission is simple:

> **Get back home.**

To achieve this, you must explore unknown star systems, gather resources, repair damaged ship systems, and survive the long journey through deep space.

---

## Features

### Ship Systems

- Reactor
- Engine
- Sonar
- Jump Drive

Each system has its own health, can break down, and must be repaired.

---

### Interactive Consoles

Operate your ship through dedicated consoles:

- Navigation Console
- Sonar Console
- Ship Status Console
- Engineering Console

---

### Resource Management

Collect valuable resources during exploration:

- Metal
- Electronics
- Fuel

Resources are required to repair and maintain the ship.

---

### Exploration

Travel through unknown star systems.

Discover:

- Asteroid fields
- Abandoned ships
- Distress signals
- Space anomalies
- Valuable resources

---

### Dynamic Damage System

Random accidents may occur during travel.

Players must:

- Locate damaged systems
- Repair them manually
- Keep the ship operational
